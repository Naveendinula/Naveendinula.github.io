About this project

Independent personal project · Sole developer and analyst

My contribution: Prepared the building-energy analysis and retrofit priorities, and built the interactive map, filters, and building-detail views.

How priorities work

Weighted scoring: ENERGY STAR (30%), Chicago Energy Rating (25%), site EUI (20%), building age (15%), and GHG intensity (10%). Missing scores appear as N/A.

3D building shapes provide geographic context. Colored markers show the retrofit priorities.
